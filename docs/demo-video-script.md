# PRE//FLIGHT — 2-minute demo video script (oud demo)

**Product:** Preflight tells brands where a campaign idea sits in real social video, whether it's early or late, and where there's still room, before they spend money producing it.
**Built at:** Oriane x Replit "Build for the Video Economy" hackathon, Dubai. Oriane = video perception (searches real Instagram/TikTok videos by speech, visuals, captions). Preflight = the decision layer. Replit = hosting + animated concept preview.

**Length:** 2:00 · **Format:** 1920×1080 screen recording + voice-over + burned-in subtitles
**Voice:** calm, confident, clear, ~140–150 words/min, no hype words. Low ambient cinematic music bed, ducked under the voice.

---

## Recording setup (screen capture)

- Chrome, full screen (F11), zoom 100%, app at `http://localhost:3000`, keep Chrome in the foreground.
- The oud demo is a real run saved earlier (160 real videos from Oriane), so it replays instantly — no waiting to cut. Do not claim it is "running live".
- Paste this exact brief (a typo triggers a different run):

> Luxury oud launch in Dubai. A creator reviews our new oud to camera, rates it on longevity and projection, and ranks it number one in their top 5 oud perfumes.

## Voice-over only (≈265 words, for recording)

Brands spend real money on creator campaigns and approve ideas on gut feeling. Nobody checks if culture already made the video.

Preflight does. Here's a real brief: a luxury oud launch in Dubai, where a creator reviews it and ranks it number one.

Preflight maps the idea into its Creative Constellation: hook, format, story, product. Then Oriane searches real Instagram and TikTok videos by what's said, seen and written.

The verdict: crowding 100. Across 160 real videos, nineteen near-identical and seventy-three close matches. And it's rising: similar videos are up 113 percent in six weeks. Globally late, but earlier in Arabic and UAE content. The insight: your look isn't the problem. Your format is.

Here's the proof: a real review with 127 thousand views, 95 percent similar. Same format, same bottle close-up, down to the exact line.

In the Creative Airspace, every screen is a real video, and our idea is the star, stuck in the densest cluster. Switch to Arabic-language content, and crowding drops to 38.

So Open Territory points the way: go Arabic-first. 38, versus 100 globally.

Reroute rewrites the idea: a street interview in a Dubai souk. "What's the most lasting oud you know?" Same brand, new format. Both ideas are scored on the same 314 videos: crowding falls from 100 to 75, and from 92 to 24 in Arabic.

And Replit turns the rerouted idea into an animated concept preview, so the team can see what to shoot.

Not a performance prediction, just less crowded in real data. Oriane sees. Preflight decides. Before you shoot, check if culture got there first.
